OUR EXPERTISE

What We Do

Five specialized divisions delivering complete solutions across engineering, energy, and agriculture.

⚡
Electrical Engineering Services
End-to-end solutions including HT/LT systems, substations, transformers, panels, cabling, testing & commissioning, energy audits, and electrical safety compliance for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.
🏗️
Civil Engineering & Construction
RCC buildings, industrial sheds, foundations, roads, drainage systems, land development, and renovation works executed with strict quality and safety standards.
☀️
Solar Energy Solutions
Complete solar EPC services including rooftop and ground-mounted plants, hybrid and off-grid systems, solar pumping, street lighting, AMC, and performance monitoring.
🌿
Agriculture & Farming
Modern farming operations cultivating vegetables and fruits using greenhouse farming, drip irrigation, organic fertilization, and scientific crop management.
🔧
Manufacturing Unit
In-house manufacturing of industrial panels and signage boards, including ACP boards, glow signs, LED displays, safety signage, control panels, AMF panels, and customized industrial solutions.
