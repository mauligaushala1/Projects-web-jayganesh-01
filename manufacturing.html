HOW WE WORK

From Site Survey to Handover

A consistent, documented process from first site visit to commissioned signage or panel.

01

Site Survey & Specification

Signage deployment depends heavily on an accurate site recce — it's the step that determines everything that follows.

  • Effective route plan so the team covers every location on site
  • Identifying the right signage format for the space available
  • Noting mounting, power and installation requirements
  • Photographic documentation used as reference for design
02

Design Translation & Signage Conceptualization

Every specification is translated into artwork and technical drawings before a single panel is cut.

  • Finalizing message content and layout for each signage type
  • Artwork adaptation to client or regulatory brand guidelines
  • Technical drawings reviewed for manufacturability
  • Reference images shared with the client for approval
03

Manufacturing & Quality Control

Fabrication happens entirely in-house, with a quality check at every stage of production.

  • ACP, acrylic, LED and metal fabrication under one roof
  • Rigorous checks on finish, colour match and durability
  • Panels tested before packaging and dispatch
  • An exclusive project team coordinating with client & site teams
04

Installation & Handover

Our own installation team mounts, wires and commissions each sign or panel on site — then verifies the result.

  • Trained field teams deployed for installation across Maharashtra
  • Adjustments accommodated on-site where needed
  • Final photographic verification at handover
  • Documentation shared with the client on completion
