In-House Production

Built for durability. Fabricated for compliance.

Our manufacturing unit produces high-quality signage boards and industrial panels designed for durability, visibility, and long-term performance. We fabricate safety, regulatory, and directional signage as per industrial norms, along with customized ACP boards, glow signs, LED display boards, and nameplates. Additionally, we manufacture various types of control panels including distribution boards, AMF panels, and customized industrial panels tailored to client needs.

Product Range

What We Manufacture

Two dedicated production lines — signage & boards, and electrical control panels — covering everything from station wayfinding to distribution equipment.

🪧

Safety & Regulatory Signage

Fire exit, emergency, hazard and compliance signage fabricated to industrial and railway safety norms for factories, stations and public infrastructure.

➡️

Directional & Wayfinding Boards

Entry/exit, platform, toilet, escalator and accessibility signage in bilingual and trilingual formats for stations, buildings and campuses.

🛗

Accessibility & Coach Signage

Divyangjan, ladies coach, and passenger-category signage produced to Indian Railways specification for rolling stock and platforms.

📋

ACP Boards & Nameplates

Custom-cut ACP (aluminium composite panel) boards, nameplates and facility signage finished for indoor and outdoor durability.

💡

Glow Signs & LED Display Boards

Illuminated glow signs and LED display boards for high-visibility branding, safety alerts and information display.

🔌

Distribution & AMF Panels

Distribution boards, AMF (Auto Mains Failure) panels, and customized industrial control panels built and tested to client specification.

How We Work

From Specification to Installation

A consistent process from first site visit to commissioned signage or panel.

1

Survey & Specification

Site survey and review of regulatory or client specification for signage type, size and mounting.

2

Design & Fabrication

In-house design proofing followed by cutting, printing and assembly at our manufacturing unit.

3

Quality Check

Every board and panel is inspected for finish, durability and compliance before it leaves the unit.

4

On-Site Installation

Our own installation team mounts, wires and commissions signage and panels on site.
