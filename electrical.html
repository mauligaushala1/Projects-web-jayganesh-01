About This Division

India's Trusted A-Class Licensed
Electrical Contractor

At Jayganesh Manohar Private Limited, our Electrical Engineering division specialises in designing, implementing, and maintaining electrical systems for industrial, commercial, and residential sectors across Maharashtra.

Our expertise spans power distribution, automation, renewable energy integration, and smart electrical solutions tailored to modern demands. We hold an A-Class Government Electrical Contractor licence and have successfully delivered projects for MSEDCL, MSETCL, Indian Railways, and major private clients.
